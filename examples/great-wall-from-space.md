# Example: real TruthTrace check (run 2026-10-20)

**Claim:** "The Great Wall of China is the only man-made object you can see from space with the naked eye."
**Verdict:** False (for the naked eye; the wall can be photographed from orbit with long lenses)
**Confidence:** High because NASA and Scientific American independently agree, and NASA documents the original photo evidence.

## Stop
Purpose: quick share check of a popular claim.

## Investigate the source
Claim circulates without a named source; it is a long-standing myth (NASA says the Moon version dates to at least 1938).

## Find better coverage
1. "China's Wall Less Great in View from Space", NASA (page last updated 2007-11-30), https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/PDFs/great_wall_less_great_from_space.pdf - the wall "generally isn't [visible], at least to the unaided eye in low Earth orbit. It certainly isn't visible from the Moon."
2. "No, You Can't See the Great Wall of China from Space", Scientific American, https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/no-you-cant-see-the-great-wall-of-china-from-space/ - the wall is too narrow and low-contrast to resolve by eye from the ISS (about 400 km up).

## Trace to the original
NASA reports the first confirmed photo of the wall from the ISS was taken by Leroy Chiao on Nov. 24 (2004) with a 180mm lens; Chiao said he did not see the wall himself. NASA image page: https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/great-wall/

## Still unverified
Whether any astronaut has reliably seen the wall unaided under ideal light: no confirmed report found.
